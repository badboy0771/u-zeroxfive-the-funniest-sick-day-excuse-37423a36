Entry for the [The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse](https://make.fun/competitions/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

One line, 138 characters:

**Can't come in — I slept on my arm wrong and it's still asleep, so technically half of me called in sick and the other half is typing this.**

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it. It validates the line first: 1–200 characters, one line, not blank — failing loudly instead of writing an entry the gate would refuse.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a reason to call in sick a boss might half believe and a coworker would laugh at; a fresh idea rather than a worn excuse about a fever, a dog or a dead grandmother; the twist arriving near the end; sounding like a real person typing a message; no wasted words. This line is all five: everyone has woken up with a dead arm, so the setup is instantly relatable; the payoff — "half of me called in sick and the other half is typing this" — is the most quotable of the three drafts tried during the build, and the joke and the reason are the same thing; the funniest words are the very last words; it reads like a typed message to a boss; and every clause does work. The two set-aside drafts and the reasoning are recorded in the comments of `main.ts`.
